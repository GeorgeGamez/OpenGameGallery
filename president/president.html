Rules

Play one, two, three, or four cards of the same rank. To beat a play, use the same number of cards at a higher rank. Pass if you cannot or do not want to beat it. Players who pass sit out until the trick resets. When everyone else passes, the last player to play leads the next trick. First to empty their hand ranks highest. After a round, Next Round deals fresh hands and exchanges cards based on standings: President gives low cards to Scum and receives high cards; in 4+ player games they exchange two, while Vice President and High Scum exchange one. Person does not exchange. Turn on Eight clears the pile in Variations for the optional eight rule. The optional Joker variation adds two cards above twos; Jokers can beat twos and are the highest rank. Toggle variations for the next deal, or use New Game to apply them immediately.
