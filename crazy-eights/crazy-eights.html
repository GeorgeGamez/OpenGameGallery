Rules

Play a card matching the top discard's rank or suit. Eights are wild and let you choose the next suit. If you cannot play, draw one card; if it can be played, you may play it immediately. First player to empty their hand wins the round. Remaining cards count as penalty points: eights = 50, face cards = 10, aces = 1, other cards = face value. Lowest total score wins when someone reaches 100.
