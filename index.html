Rules

Play one, two, three, or four cards of the same rank. To beat a play, use the same number of cards at a higher rank. Pass if you cannot or do not want to beat it. Players who pass sit out until the trick resets. When everyone else passes, the last player to play leads the next trick. First to empty their hand ranks highest; the last player holding cards ranks lowest.
