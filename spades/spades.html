Rules (starting version)

Four players in fixed partnerships. Each player bids tricks, then must follow suit if possible. Spades are trump and cannot be led until broken unless a player only has spades. A made bid scores 10 points per bid trick plus 1 per extra trick (bag); a failed bid loses 10 points per bid trick. Every 10 bags costs 100 points. First team to 500 points wins. Nil bids, blind nil, and online play are not included yet.
