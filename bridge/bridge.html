How to Play

North–South partner against East–West. Bids promise a level of at least seven tricks through thirteen; clubs, diamonds, hearts, spades, and no-trump increase in rank. After three passes following a bid, the highest bid becomes the contract. The player to declarer's left leads, then dummy's cards are revealed. Follow suit when possible; otherwise any card may be played. A suit trump beats cards in other suits. This alpha uses simplified computer bidding and play, with duplicate-style scoring.
