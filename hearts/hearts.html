Rules

Avoid penalty cards: each heart is worth 1 point and the queen of spades is worth 13. Lowest total score wins when a player reaches 100. Follow suit if possible. The 2♣ starts the first trick; hearts cannot be led until broken, and point cards cannot be played on the first trick unless you have no alternative. Passing rotates left, right, across, then no passing.
